Inside Our Boutique

Our Story

A Boutique Built on
50 Years of Craft

Founded over half a century ago in George Town, Penang, Butik Bee Gee has dedicated itself to preserving and sharing the beauty of Peranakan fashion. We are not a mass retailer — every piece in our boutique is handcrafted, curated, and available in limited quantities.

Our mission is to keep traditional artistry alive — from the intricate embroidery of Kebaya Nyonya to the delicate beadwork of Nyonya shoes. We serve customers locally in Penang and ship internationally, offering a truly boutique experience through personalized consultation.
